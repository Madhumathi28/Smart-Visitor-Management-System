# Concepts Learned

## Form

A Form is used to create, view, or update a single record in ServiceNow.

Example:
One Visitor Record

---

## List

A List displays multiple records in a table.

Example:
List of all Visitors

---

## Form Designer

The Form Designer allows developers to customize how records appear to users by arranging fields in a logical order.

---

## Field Layout

A well-organized form helps users enter information quickly and reduces mistakes.

Fields should be grouped based on their purpose and business process.

# Challenges Faced

The newer ServiceNow Studio uses a different Form Designer than many tutorials.

Initially, editing the form was not possible because it opened in read-only mode.

The issue was resolved by selecting **Edit in original scope**, which enabled drag-and-drop editing.

---

# Best Practices

- Keep frequently used fields at the top.
- Group related information together.
- Avoid unnecessary fields on the form.
- Use meaningful field labels.
- Design forms from the end user's perspective.
- Test the form after every major change.
